The dew point gap

One number. Room dew point minus outdoor dew point. It tells you whether your ventilation is the bottleneck or the weather is. Relative humidity cannot answer this, because RH changes with temperature and dew point does not.

Read the Sonoff THS01 in eWeLink. The app number is the true number (−1.5 °C offset already applied).

How to read it. Outdoor dew point is the hard floor on how dry your room air can ever be. Ventilation moves the room towards it and can never go past it. A gap above 1.5 °C means your birds, drinkers and trapped air are adding moisture faster than the exhaust removes it, so the fix is airflow. A gap near zero means the exhaust has already done everything it can and the only remaining levers are room temperature and the calendar.

The Bangla month outlook

Climate normals, not forecast. The Bangladesh civil calendar is solar and locked to the Gregorian since the Bangla Academy 2019 revision, so every month below always falls on the same English dates. Room temp for band is the room temperature that lands VPD at 12 hPa, the middle of the incubation band.

Read the fourth column carefully. Holding the room at 30.5 °C is correct in Bhadro and Ashwin and becomes the thing that kills your hatch from Kartik onward. Heating dry Hemonto and Sheet air to 30.5 °C drives VPD past 20 hPa, well beyond the 15 ceiling, which shrink-wraps chicks at pipping. From Kartik you let the room fall, you do not chase a number.
