AC SOP · Room 1 and Room 2

The goal: keep temperature and humidity stable day and night, so pairs lay quickly, birds stay healthy and eggs hatch. Follow the 11 steps in order. After step 9 the room runs by itself. The only daily job is refilling the humidifier water.

Why stable matters

Quick laying

Above 32 °C hens lay less and the male's sperm is damaged for 10 to 14 days. A steady 27 °C removes that brake.

Healthy birds

No heat stress, no cold drafts, fresh air every night, no mould. Panting birds eat less and breed less.

High hatch

An egg must lose 13 to 16% of its weight by day 21. Too dry and the chick sticks in the shell. 58 to 68% keeps it right.

Your Room 1, 11 to 17 Oct: above 32 °C for 14.7 hours a day, too dry for 77% of hours. This page fixes that.

1

Call Walton before you pay

Do this

Call 16267. Ask: "Is a bird room in my home covered as residential use?" Get the answer by SMS or email.

What it does

Walton's warranty excludes commercial use and ammonia damage. Bird droppings give off ammonia.

Why it matters for your birds

Keeps the 12-year compressor warranty. If they say commercial, buy the Gree Lomo (copper anti-corrosion coil) instead.

2

Order the AC

Do this

Walton 1.5 ton inverter, WSI-INVERNA (SUPERSAVER)-18H Plasma, ৳60,690 on Pickaboo. Ask: is EMI 0% on ৳60,690? If not, pay cash.

What it does

An inverter slows down instead of switching on and off, so the room stays steady. 1.5 ton covers both rooms through the connecting door.

Why it matters for your birds

Steady 27 °C keeps males fertile and hens laying on time.

3

Buy the small parts

Do this

Tuya IR remote with sensor ৳1,850 (SmartX BD, test in the shop) · 5 V USB adapter ~৳300 · Tuya smart plug 16A ৳990 (RoboticsBD) · Humidifier parts ~৳2,000 · Sealing kit ~৳4,000 (Nawabpur). Total with AC ≈ ৳69,800.

What it does

The remote is the brain: it reads the air at cage height and gives the AC its orders. The plug switches the humidifier.

Why it matters for your birds

Without the brain someone presses buttons all day. With it the room runs itself.

4

Seal the room

Do this

Tick each item below. 1 to 3 days of work.

What it does

Closes every hole where hot air gets in and cool air gets out.

Why it matters for your birds

The AC holds 27 °C with less work, a smaller bill and steadier air.

0 of 10 done. Ticks are shared with the Weather page.

5

Installation day

Do this

Show the installer: Room 1, wall nearest the connecting door · blow along the room above cage height · louvre to the ceiling · never at cages or boxes · short pipe, sealed hole · outdoor unit in shade · own breaker · test auto-restart (breaker off, on).

What it does

Puts the cold air where it cools the room without touching the birds.

Why it matters for your birds

Cold drafts on nest boxes kill embryos. The most likely reason one friend's AC room has dead-in-shell.

6

Set up the remote and plug

Do this

Remote on its USB adapter at cage height, facing the AC. Add it in Smart Life on your Deco WiFi. Teach it the Walton remote if Walton is not listed. Add the plug; set Relay status after power on = Memory.

What it does

The remote must see the AC and read the air the birds breathe.

Why it matters for your birds

A sensor near the ceiling reads 1 to 2 °C hotter than the birds feel and gives wrong orders.

7

Create the 9 automations

Do this

Smart Life: Scene → + → When device status changes → the remote → temperature or humidity → set the number → Then → the AC or the plug.

What it does

These rules are the autopilot.

Why it matters for your birds

Temperature and humidity stay in range when nobody is home.

Do this

eWeLink phone app, on both R1 and R2 Exhaust (the website cannot edit without a paid plan).

What it does

Why it matters for your birds

A sealed AC room builds ammonia and CO2, which hurt bird lungs.

9

Build and start the humidifier

Do this

Lidded tub, 3 to 4 cm of water · cooler pad standing in it · small fan blowing through the pad · fan switch left ON · plugged into the smart plug · centre of Room 1, out of reach, not facing a box.

What it does

Adds water to the air only when too dry, and stops when it is enough.

Why it matters for your birds

Holds 58 to 68%, where eggs lose the right amount of water and chicks hatch.

10

Every day

Do this

Refill the humidifier water (you or your brother). Glance at Smart Life: 26 to 28 °C and 58 to 68%.

What it does

Keeps the autopilot fed.

Why it matters for your birds

An empty tank dries the room within hours.

11

Every week and month

Do this

Weekly: AC Frost Clean, scrub the tub, send R1 and R2 history to Claude. Every 2 weeks: clean the AC filter. Monthly: vinegar soak the pad, check shutters. Every 6 months: salt test the sensors.

What it does

Keeps every part working.

Why it matters for your birds

A clogged filter or mouldy pad slowly undoes everything above.

AC not cooling
You seeWarm air from the unit
Do thisClean the filter first, then call 16267.
Birds panting at 27 °C
You seeWings held out, beak open
Do thisCheck the sensor is at cage height and air reaches that cage. Move the bird down a tier.

Never

  • Never point the AC at cages or nest boxes.
  • Never use Dry mode; the room is already too dry.
  • Never set below 25 °C.
  • Never an ultrasonic humidifier in a bird room.
  • Never run the exhaust continuously while the AC runs (only the 5-minute pulses).
  • Never spray chlorine, bleach or ammonia cleaners near the indoor unit.
  • Never wet the floor.
  • Never skip the daily water refill.
