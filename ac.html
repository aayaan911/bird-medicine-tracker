Why this exists

Target: 8 pairs × 2 clutches × 3 babies = 48 babies × ৳58,100 average ≈ ৳27.9 lakh. The 80/20 of hitting it:

4Fosters to take eggs so hens re-lay~10%Wave 2 fosters

Shares are estimates. Measured: 11 to 17 Oct Room 1 was above 32 °C for 14.7 hours a day and too dry for 77% of hours. Studies: hens lay less at 32 °C than at 22 to 27 °C, and heat-damaged sperm is slow to recover.

The 24-hour cycle

The AC does the day, outside air does the night. Same 27 °C for the birds, lower bill, fresh air every night.

TimeACExhaustWhy
09:00 to 10:00OFFOFFShort break
10:00 to 19:00Cool 27 °C, fan low, louvre upOFF, plus 5-minute fresh-air pulses 11:00, 13:00, 15:00, 17:00Outside is hotter than the room by day
19:00 to 19:15Fan modeOFFDries the coil so mould cannot grow
19:15 to 09:00OFF (guard rule turns it on above 29 °C)ON, south window openOutside nights are cooler: free cooling and fresh air

If 04:00 room temperature stays above 28 °C for 3 nights, switch to AC 24/7 at 27 °C with the exhaust pulsing 20 minutes every 3 hours. As Ogrohayon and Poush cool, AC hours shrink by themselves.

Smart Life automations (set once)

All rules use the Tuya IR remote's own temperature and humidity reading. Sensor accuracy ±1 °C, ±5% humidity, which is why the humidity gap is 10 points wide.

#RuleWhy
1IF humidity < 58% → smart plug ON (humidifier)Adds moisture when dry
2IF humidity > 68% → smart plug OFFStops before it gets too wet. The gap stops it flicking on and off
3Schedule 10:00 → AC Cool 27 °C, fan lowDaytime cooling
4Schedule 19:00 → AC Fan mode; 19:15 → AC OFFCoil dry, mould prevention
5IF temp > 29 °C, effective 19:15 to 09:00 → AC Cool 27 °CHot-night guard
6IF temp > 30 °C, effective 10:00 to 19:00 → AC Cool 27 °CRe-sends ON after a power cut
7IF temp > 32 °C → phone notificationMale fertility limit
8IF humidity < 50% → phone notificationHumidifier tank probably empty
9IF humidity > 75% → phone notificationToo wet, mould risk
How to build one rule in Smart Life
  1. Tap Scene → + → When device status changes.
  2. Pick the IR remote → Humidity (or Temperature) → choose < or > → enter the number.
  3. Then: pick the smart plug → Switch ON / OFF (or the AC → mode, 27 °C).
  4. Optional: Effective period for rules 5 and 6.
  5. Save. Repeat for each rule.

Smart plug setting: Relay status after power on = Memory. Walton not in the IR brand list? Use DIY learning mode and teach each button from the Walton remote. Pairing: Tuya needs 2.4 GHz; if it fails on the Deco M4, pair it on the Deco guest network or switch 5 GHz off for 2 minutes.

DIY evaporative humidifier

Evaporative only. Ultrasonic sprays tap-water minerals and germs into the air the birds breathe. A plain fan starts the moment the plug gives power, so it works with rules 1 and 2.

StepDetail
1. TubLidded plastic tub, 3 to 4 cm of water
2. PadHoneycomb cellulose cooler pad standing upright in the water. It wicks water up, no pump
3. FanSmall 220 V fan (or USB fan on an adapter) blowing through the pad, switch left ON, plugged into the smart plug
4. PlaceCentre of Room 1, out of reach of birds, never blowing at a nest box
OutputAbout 2.5 L/day with a 120 mm fan, up to ~6 L/day with a stronger fan. Slows as the room gets wetter
HygieneChange water every 2 to 3 days. Scrub the tub weekly. Soak the pad in diluted vinegar monthly, rinse well. Replace the pad every ~90 days

Empty-tank check: the plug shows watts. If the plug is ON, the fan runs but humidity keeps falling below 50%, the pad is dry: refill.

Never

  • Never point the AC at cages or nest boxes.
  • Never use Dry mode. The room is already too dry.
  • Never set below 25 °C. 26 to 28 °C is the target.
  • Never an ultrasonic humidifier in a bird room.
  • Never run the exhaust continuously while the AC runs (only the 5-minute pulses).
  • Never spray chlorine, bleach or ammonia cleaners near the indoor unit (warranty exclusion and bird lungs).
  • Never wet the floor.
  • Never skip the daily water refill.

Pro tips

  1. Call 16267 about residential use before ordering, and keep the reply.
  2. Book installation for the same week; start sealing today with the tick list above.
  3. Test the Tuya remote on the shop counter before paying.
  4. Set the smart plug to Memory on day one.
  5. Build the DIY humidifier this week: the hatch is running now.
  6. Use solid-wood nest boxes and natural nesting material, never newspaper (it pulls water out of eggs).
  7. Weigh eggs on a 0.001 g scale: 13 to 16% loss by day 21 means humidity is right.
  8. After install, send screenshots of every Smart Life rule for a check.
