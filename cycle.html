When each thing starts working

Vitamin E cannot be rushed. It builds new sperm over a 14-day cycle. L-carnitine works on sperm that already exists, so it acts in days. That is why the course runs Fetura 12 first for 7 days, the fast lever, then Ferti-Vit daily until the first egg, the deep one with the selenium vitamin E needs.

Humidity emergency: the wet towel

Free backup instead of buying a humidifier. Test it once in November so you know it works before you need it.

Why it works. Wet clothes on a line dry because the water leaves the cloth and goes into the air. It does not vanish, it becomes invisible vapour. A dehumidifier takes water OUT of air. A wet towel puts water IN. Same idea, opposite direction, and one is free.

The setup. Hang a wet towel in front of the air inlet with a bowl underneath to catch drips. The exhaust pulls fresh air past it, that air picks up water on the way in, so it arrives wetter. Wetter air is less thirsty air, so the egg loses water more slowly.
