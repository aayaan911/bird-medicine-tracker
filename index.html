Three rules before any dose

Most supplement damage in an aviary comes from stacking and from timing, not from underdosing. These three rules prevent almost all of it.

Never combine on the same day

  • Two fertility products, such as Fetura 12 with Ferti-Vit, Nekton E or Nekton Breed Star
  • Two vitamin D3 products. Calciform, Calcivet, Avigold Advance, Breed Star and A21 all carry D3
  • Two electrolyte products, such as Spark Liquid with Nekton Elektrolyt or Prolyte-C
  • Any probiotic during a course of medication
Stop at the first egg. Nekton E and Nekton Breed Star are pre breeding products. Nekton's own label says to stop the day the first egg is laid, because continuing drives the male too hard and disrupts incubation. Running them through incubation is a known way to lose a clutch.
Water rule. Medicated water must be the only water source, mixed fresh every day, and left in the cage no longer than six hours, or three hours on a hot afternoon. Doses below are the manufacturer figures, converted to a 1 litre basis where the label used 250 ml or 500 ml.
More protocols. The full weekly and monthly calendar, the immunity programme, the herb formula, feeding times and the dead-egg diagnostic table live on the notes page.
Act within the hour

Emergency actions

Five situations that decide whether a bird recovers or is lost. Learn these before you need them.

Leg paralysis

Quiet cage, perches removed, food and water at floor level. Liquid calcium by syringe into the beak, repeated after two hours. B complex, electrolyte for hydration, warmth and no disturbance. Most laying related cases resolve within two to three days once calcium is restored.

Weak or fluffed bird

Warmth first, always. A cold bird cannot recover no matter what you give it. Then electrolyte solution in water and warm boiled egg mash. If there is no improvement within two days, isolate the bird and reassess.

Loose droppings

Stop every supplement for 24 hours and give plain water with a little vinegar. From day two, run a probiotic for five days. Electrolyte for a maximum of five days. If droppings are bloody or continue past two days, escalate to a vet.

Heat emergency

Run every fan at once, then check nest boxes for hens off their eggs. Above 35 degrees, wet the floor for evaporative cooling. Electrolyte solution in the drinkers for the rest of the day.

A new bird arrives

Four weeks of quarantine, in a separate room, handled last every day. Week one is electrolyte only. Week two adds a daily tonic and light soft food. Weeks three and four cover the quarantine medication course. Never shorten it.

One pair, one rotation

The breeding course

Timing matters more than the products. A male's sperm cycle runs 10 to 14 days, and Nekton asks for three weeks of preparation, so the fertility window opens well before the box goes in.
