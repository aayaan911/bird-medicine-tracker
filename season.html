The uncomfortable rule. Plan on your realised average from last season, never on your best sale. One expensive pair can look like a whole season at the top of the ladder. That is exactly why it must never set the plan. If that pair throws clear eggs, a plan built on it is finished. A plan built on the volume lane barely notices.
Where the gap usually is. Most breeders chase fertility with more supplements. Fertility at 90% is near the biological ceiling. The number that moves whole seasons is hatch of fertile, and it is decided by nest box humidity, calcium and D3, and zero disturbance after day 18, not by anything in a bottle. Change the hatch field from 80 to 90 and watch the eggs column.
