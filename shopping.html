How to read break even. If you spent 12 lakh on stock and your realised average is 75,000 per baby, the first 16 babies you sell pay for the birds. Everything after that is the season's return. Count babies, not clutches, and count them at the price you actually get, not the price you hope for.










  

    Three rules
    
What the total is trying to tell you

    
A shopping list is only useful if it changes a decision. These are the three it should change.

  

  

    
Every new bird goes through four weeks of quarantine
The most expensive bird on this list is the one most likely to carry something in. Price does not buy health.

    
A bird that has not produced by its third clutch is a decision you delayed
The purchase price is gone either way. Only the cage space is still yours to recover.

    
Never plan the season on the top row of the price ladder
Break even uses your realised average. If you have to raise the sale price to make the list look better, the list is telling you something.
